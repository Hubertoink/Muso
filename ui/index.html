          Image generation

          4K generates 3840 × 2160 images and costs more per image.

          New research can use up to five web searches. Muso saves the findings and writes a missing article when generating images with research on. Saved research and articles also guide images when web research is off.

          Every image is reviewed. A repaint adds one paid high-quality generation and another review. Scenes that fail the publication criteria are skipped.

          How images are generated

          AI choice can use the cover to choose a visual style for the series. Scene review compares the plan with the cover and up to six earlier paintings. Each image is generated with the full series plan and checked against the cover and its musical cue. Muso saves the images locally at your selected resolution.
